## The Real Goal

Do not study to sound smart.

Study to be able to explain:

- what you built
- why you chose the stack
- what tradeoffs you made
- how the system works end to end
- what broke
- how you fixed it
- what AI improved
- what you would do differently

If you cannot explain those clearly, you are not ready.
