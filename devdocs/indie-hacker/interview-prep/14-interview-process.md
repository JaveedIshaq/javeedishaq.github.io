# Interview Process Guide

## Before Applying

### You Need

- ATS-safe resume
- tailored resume per role
- clean LinkedIn
- 2 to 3 strong case studies
- clear GitHub or portfolio proof

### Your Resume Must Show

- Product Engineer identity
- Flutter depth
- Next.js / NestJS / PostgreSQL / Supabase credibility
- practical AI integration

## Before Each Interview

Research:

- company product
- user type
- business model
- likely architecture shape
- what your experience matches

Prepare:

- 90-second intro
- 3 core project stories
- 2 failure stories
- 1 system-design story
- 1 AI integration story

## Interview Rounds

### Recruiter / Screening

Focus:

- clear positioning
- why this stack
- why this company
- communication quality

### Technical Interview

Focus:

- JavaScript / TypeScript fundamentals
- React / Next.js understanding
- backend architecture
- auth / data / API reasoning

### System Design

Focus:

- breaking problems down
- tradeoffs
- scale and reliability
- security and permissions
- operational awareness

### Behavioral

Focus:

- ownership
- ambiguity
- collaboration
- failure recovery
- scope control

## Tell Me About Yourself Structure

Use this pattern:

1. current role and strength
2. broader stack and shift
3. what you are building now
4. why you fit this role

Example structure:

`I’m a product engineer with deep Flutter experience, and over time I’ve been deliberately expanding into the TypeScript full-stack ecosystem with Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, and Supabase. I’ve worked across mobile, backend, and admin workflows, and my current focus is shipping full-stack product systems with practical AI features. I’m targeting roles where that end-to-end ownership is useful rather than being limited to a single platform.`

## Practice Questions

- Why Product Engineer?
- Why this stack?
- Why NestJS over Express?
- When would you use Supabase instead of a custom backend?
- Tell me about a feature you shipped end to end.
- Tell me about a system-design decision you defended.
- How do you use AI in products responsibly?

## Final Rule

Do not try to impress with terminology.

Win by being:

- clear
- technical
- specific
- honest about tradeoffs
- grounded in shipped work
