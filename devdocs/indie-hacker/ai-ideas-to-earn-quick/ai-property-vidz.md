### Video Summary (Point Wise)

**Topic:** *Easiest Way to Make $2000/Month – Selling Virtual Property Tours*

1. Real estate agents often have many property listings.
2. Most agents do **not have virtual tours** for their properties.
3. You can create virtual property tours for them as a service.
4. Use software/tools that convert property photos into a 3D or interactive virtual tour.
5. The process is mostly automated and does not require advanced technical skills.
6. You collect property images from the agent.
7. Upload the images into the virtual-tour software.
8. The software generates a virtual walkthrough/tour.
9. Share the tour link with the real estate agent.
10. The agent can use the link in property listings and marketing.

### Business Model

* Find real estate agents.
* Offer virtual tour creation as a service.
* Charge per property or a monthly package.
* Repeat the process for multiple properties.

### Why the Creator Thinks It Works

* Helps agents market homes better.
* Makes listings look more professional.
* Saves buyers time by allowing online viewing.
* Can be delivered remotely.

### My Honest Opinion

✅ **Real service with genuine demand**

✅ Easier to sell than many AI services because agents immediately understand the value.

✅ Low startup cost.

⚠️ Income depends heavily on finding clients, not creating the tours.

⚠️ In Pakistan, demand is lower than in countries like Australia, Canada, the US, and the UK.

⚠️ Getting your first paying client will be the hardest part.
