### What the video is teaching

The creator is explaining **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**:

1. Take PDFs/documents.
2. Convert text into embeddings.
3. Store embeddings in a vector database (ChromaDB, Pinecone, etc.).
4. Retrieve relevant chunks.
5. Send them to ChatGPT/LLM.
6. Get answers from company documents.

This is a real AI engineering concept and not a scam.
