### Every Project
- `Flutter` for mobile apps
- `Next.js` for landing pages, product web apps, and SEO-facing surfaces
- `React/Next.js` for admin dashboards and internal tools
- `AI APIs` for practical AI features

- It preserves your strongest edge: Flutter for mobile delivery.
- It is fast for solo shipping and credible for startup hiring.

- `pgvector` is already available in Supabase and PostgreSQL — no new infrastructure needed.
- It gives you a believable identity in the market: product engineer with mobile depth and practical AI capability.
