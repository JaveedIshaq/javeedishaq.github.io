# AI Context Switch Meeting Assistant
### Product Concept for a Solo Product Engineer

> **Simple promise:** Turn meeting notes into project-specific action items automatically so multi-project workers lose less context.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

Meetings are not just noisy.
They are disruptive.

For people juggling multiple projects, the real pain is not transcription alone.
It is:

- which project this belongs to
- what the next step is
- where it should go

This product focuses on context-aware meeting outputs.

---

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary users:

- freelancers
- consultants
- startup operators
- product managers
- agency teams

Secondary users:

- small remote teams using Notion, Trello, Jira, or ClickUp

---

## 3. The Problem

Meeting assistants usually summarize.
They do not sort action items into the right work context.

Users still have to manually organize:

- project ownership
- board placement
- next actions

---

## 4. Product Promise

### One-line version

- Turn meetings into project-ready tasks and action items automatically.

### Stronger version

- Record or upload meeting audio and get action items organized by project context, ready for your task tools.

---

## 5. Core Product Outcome

The user should receive:

- summary
- action items
- project categorization
- owner suggestions
- export to project tools

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### MVP input

- meeting audio or transcript
- project labels/workspaces

### MVP output

- concise summary
- categorized action items
- due date suggestions
- export-ready structured output

### MVP workflows

1. User uploads transcript or audio
2. AI summarizes and extracts tasks
3. AI maps tasks to selected project contexts
4. User exports results

---

## 7. Feature Roadmap

### Version 1

- transcript import
- summary generation
- action item extraction
- project tagging
- export to markdown or CSV

### Version 2

- Notion/Jira/Trello integration
- recurring meeting memory
- participant role mapping
- smarter due-date logic

### Version 3

- live meeting mode
- team collaboration
- cross-project workload insights

---

## 8. Why This Product Could Work

This is more differentiated than another generic meeting summarizer.

Its wedge is:

- context organization

not just:

- transcription

That is useful for multi-project workers.

---

## 9. Monetization

- subscription

## 10. Best Stack for You

- `Next.js` web-first
- `Supabase` for users/projects/history
- transcription provider
- LLM summarization pipeline

Launch this as a web tool, not mobile-first.
The workflow is more natural on desktop.

## 11. Real Risks

- crowded meeting assistant market
- transcription cost pressure
- integration complexity

So the positioning must stay narrow:

- for multi-project professionals

---

## 12. Best First Positioning

### Weak

- AI meeting summarizer

### Better

- project-aware meeting assistant

### Stronger

- stop losing project context after meetings and turn conversations into organized next steps
