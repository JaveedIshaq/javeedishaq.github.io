# AI‑Powered Pet Emotion Detection Flutter App | Pet Mood Tracker

**Overview**
- A Flutter application designed to analyze pet facial expressions and behavior to determine their emotional state.
- Built with **Dart** (Flutter) for Android and iOS.
- Offers a "Pet Mood Tracker" and "Behavior Analysis" powered by AI (Google Gemini).

**Key Features**
- **AI Emotion Detection** – Scans pet photos to detect emotions (e.g., happy, sad, angry, relaxed) and provide behavioral insights.
- **Pet Details & Health** – Stores pet profiles including breed, age, and vaccination records.
- **Detailed Insights** – Explains *why* the pet might be feeling a certain way and suggests appropriate interactions.
- **Care Routines** – Automated daily/weekly schedules for feeding, play, and grooming based on mood analysis.
- **Pet Diary** – Journal for owners to track mood changes over time.
- **Chatbot Assistant** – AI‑powered chat for pet care advice and interpreting behavior.
- **Offline Access** – Locally stored history and reports for access without data/Wi‑Fi.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter 3.x).
- **AI Model:** Uses Google Gemini API for multimodal analysis (image + text).
- **UI Design:** Modern, playful interface with smooth animations and theme customization.
- **Data Storage:** Local storage (Hive/SQLite) for privacy and offline capability.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. specific: Add your Google Gemini API key to `lib/config/api_keys.dart` (or similar config file).
4. Run `flutter pub get` and launch with `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Expand emotion categories with custom training data for specific breeds.
- Add sound analysis to interpret barks/meows.
- Integrate social sharing to post "Pet Mood of the Day" cards.
