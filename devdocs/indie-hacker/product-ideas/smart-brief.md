# Smart‑Brief: AI‑Driven Meeting Summaries

| **Section** | **Details** |
|---|---|
| **App Name** | **Smart‑Brief** |
| **Core Problem** | Professionals waste time reviewing meeting recordings or notes to extract decisions, action items, and key takeaways. |
| **AI Capability Used** | Speech‑to‑text transcription (Whisper) + LLM summarization (Claude/ChatGPT) + keyword extraction for action items. |
| **Target Users** | Remote teams, sales reps, project managers, freelancers who record meetings on Zoom/Teams or phone calls. |
| **Key Features (MVP)** | 1️⃣ Record or import audio/video → auto‑transcribe.<br>2️⃣ Generate concise summary (≤ 200 words).<br>3️⃣ Highlight action items with due dates.<br>4️⃣ Export to Markdown/Google Docs.<br>5️⃣ Share via a one‑click link. |
| **Why AI Makes This Work** | Accurate transcription + contextual summarization is impossible without AI; manual note‑taking is error‑prone and time‑consuming. |
| **Monetization Strategy** | • **Freemium**: 5 minutes of transcription per month free.<br>• **Subscription** $9.99 /mo for unlimited minutes, priority processing, and export integrations.<br>• **Pay‑as‑you‑go** for extra transcription minutes ($0.02 /min). |
| **Marketing & Growth Angle** | • Partnerships with remote‑work tools (Slack, Notion).<br>• Content marketing on “how to get more out of meetings”.<br>• Referral credits for shared meeting links. |
| **Why Now?** | 2025‑2026 sees massive adoption of hybrid work, improved on‑device transcription, and cheaper LLM APIs, making high‑quality summarization affordable for SMBs. |
