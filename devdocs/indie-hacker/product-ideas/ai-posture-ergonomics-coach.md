# AI Posture & Ergonomics Coach
### Product Concept for a Solo Product Engineer

> **Simple promise:** Help desk workers and remote professionals improve posture and ergonomics before discomfort becomes pain.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

Millions of people sit badly for hours and only react when pain starts.

A posture app should not just nag.
It should help people build healthier desk habits with minimal friction.

---

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary users:

- remote workers
- developers
- designers
- students
- desk-heavy professionals

Secondary users:

- employers offering wellness tools
- freelancers with long sitting hours

---

## 3. The Problem

Most people do not notice bad posture in real time.

They need:

- simple feedback
- ergonomic reminders
- desk setup guidance
- habit reinforcement

---

## 4. Product Promise

### One-line version

- Improve posture and desk habits with real-time ergonomic guidance.

### Stronger version

- Use camera-assisted coaching and smart ergonomic reminders to reduce poor posture during work and study sessions.

---

## 5. Core Product Outcome

The user should get:

- posture feedback
- break reminders
- ergonomic setup advice
- progress tracking

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### MVP input

- self-assessment
- optional camera posture check
- work session duration

### MVP output

- ergonomic checklist
- posture alerts
- movement reminders
- improvement score

### MVP workflows

1. User sets up profile and work pattern
2. App checks posture manually or periodically
3. App gives lightweight nudges

---

## 7. Feature Roadmap

### Version 1

- onboarding and self-assessment
- desk setup guidance
- timed reminders
- posture check via guided camera capture
- history of sessions

### Version 2

- real-time monitoring
- stretch routines
- personalized coaching
- wearable integration

### Version 3

- corporate dashboards
- team wellness reporting
- occupational health partnerships

---

## 8. Why This Product Could Work

The problem is common, recurring, and easy to explain.

This is stronger as:

- a wellness and ergonomics tool

than as a pseudo-medical app.

---

## 9. Monetization

- freemium
- premium coaching
- team/corporate plan

---

## 10. Best Stack for You

- `Flutter` mobile app
- optional on-device pose detection later
- local-first reminders

### Honest recommendation

Start with guided checks and ergonomic routines, not full real-time AI posture tracking.

That keeps the first version shippable.

---

## 11. Real Risks

- camera monitoring can feel creepy
- poor detection creates annoyance
- medical overclaiming is dangerous

So position it as habit improvement, not diagnosis.

---

## 12. Best First Positioning

### Weak

- AI posture app

### Better

- posture and desk ergonomics coach

### Stronger

- fix small desk habits before they become daily pain

---

## 13. Recommendation

This is a good idea if kept simple.

Do not start with full-time camera surveillance.
Start with:

- assessments
- guided checks
- break nudges
- ergonomic improvements
