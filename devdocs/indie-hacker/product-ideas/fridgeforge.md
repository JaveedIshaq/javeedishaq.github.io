# FridgeForge — Turn Leftovers Into Culinary Gold

## Core Problem
Households waste 30-40% of food due to forgotten leftovers, poor meal planning, and lack of inspiration for repurposing ingredients. This leads to higher grocery bills and significant environmental impact — especially critical amid rising food costs.

## AI/Tech Capability Used
- **On-Device ML Kit** — Camera-based ingredient scanning (TensorFlow Lite, ML Kit)
- **LLM** — Recipe generation and substitution logic
- **Vision API** — Food item recognition and freshness estimation
- **Recommendation Engine** — Personalized recipe suggestions based on available ingredients

## Target Users
- Busy urban millennials (25-40 years old)
- Young families cooking at home
- Cost-conscious consumers in US, Europe, and emerging economies
- Sustainability-minded individuals
- Anyone struggling with meal creativity and food waste

## Key Features (MVP)

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Ingredient Scanner | Camera-based fridge/pantry inventory using device ML |
| Smart Recipes | AI-suggested recipes from available ingredients |
| Shopping List | Auto-generated lists to avoid overbuying |
| Expiry Tracking | Alerts before food goes bad |
| Waste Dashboard | Track money saved and waste reduced |

## Post-MVP Features
- User-submitted recipe sharing community
- Nutritional tracking integration
- Gamified challenges ("Zero-Waste Week" streaks)
- Meal prep planning for the week
- Integration with smart fridges

## Why This Works Now
**Without tech:** Manual tracking, forgotten items, recipe searching fatigue.

**With AI:**
- Point camera at fridge → instant inventory
- "Here's 5 recipes from what's expiring first"
- "You're about to overbuy chicken — you have some in the freezer"
- Weekly waste report with savings calculated
