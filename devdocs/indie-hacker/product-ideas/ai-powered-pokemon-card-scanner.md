# AI‑Powered Pokémon Card Scanner Flutter App

https://codecanyon.net/item/aipowered-pokmon-card-scanner-flutter-app-trading-card-identifier/60925661?s_rank=15

**Overview**
- A commercial Flutter app that scans and identifies Pokémon trading cards using AI.
- Built with **Dart** for Android and iOS platforms.
- Provides card scanning, result analysis, history tracking, collection management, and an AI chat assistant.

**Key Features**
- **Card Scanning** – Use device camera to capture card images and obtain instant identification.
- **Result Analysis** – AI‑driven recognition of card name, rarity, set, and other metadata.
- **History & Analytics** – View past scans, statistics on collection composition, and performance dashboards.
- **Collection Management** – Organize cards into custom sets, mark favorites, and track duplicates.
- **AI Pokémon Chat Assistant** – Conversational assistant that can answer card‑related questions.
- **Customization** – Clean, modular codebase with easy theming, color, text, and graphic adjustments.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter 3.x compatible)
- **UI Design:** Modern, clean layout with gradient accents and micro‑animations.
- **Dependencies:** Standard Flutter packages plus AI inference SDK (e.g., TensorFlow Lite or cloud‑based model endpoint).
- **Supported Devices:** Android phones/tablets and iPhone/iPad.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. Run `flutter pub get` to install dependencies.
4. Connect a device or start an emulator and execute `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Replace the AI model with a custom endpoint or on‑device model.
