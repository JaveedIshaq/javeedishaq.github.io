# AI Garden Health Diagnostic
### Product Concept for a Solo Product Engineer

> **Simple promise:** Scan a plant in your garden and get practical help for visible health issues before it gets worse.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

Home gardeners often know something is wrong but not:

- what it is
- how urgent it is
- what to do next

This product sits closer to plant care and garden recovery than general plant identification.

---

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary users:

- home gardeners
- balcony plant owners
- hobby growers
- small-scale vegetable gardeners

Secondary users:

- nursery customers
- garden centers

---

## 3. The Problem

Users see:

- yellow leaves
- brown edges
- pests
- weak growth

but struggle to diagnose whether the cause is:

- watering
- nutrients
- disease
- pests
- light problems

---

## 4. Product Promise

### One-line version

- Diagnose common visible plant problems and get a simple recovery plan.

### Stronger version

- Scan leaves and garden plants to detect likely health issues, understand probable causes, and get step-by-step care guidance.

---

## 5. Core Product Outcome

The user should receive:

- likely issue category
- confidence level
- possible causes
- simple recovery steps

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### MVP input

- plant photo
- optional plant type
- optional symptoms checklist

### MVP output

- likely problem class
- urgency note
- simple next steps
- care reminders

### MVP workflows

1. User uploads plant image
2. System analyzes visible symptoms
3. App returns likely cause and recovery advice

---

## 7. Feature Roadmap

### Version 1

- photo analysis
- issue categories
- suggested treatment steps
- saved scan history

### Version 2

- plant-specific care plans
- pest library
- seasonal garden reminders
- treatment tracking

### Version 3

- local garden expert upsell
- product recommendations
- community Q&A

---

## 8. Why This Product Could Work

This is stronger than generic plant identification if you focus on:

- "what is wrong"

instead of only:

- "what plant is this"

That makes it more urgent and potentially more monetizable.

---

## 9. Monetization

- premium diagnosis history
- care plan subscription
- affiliate gardening supplies

---

## 10. Best Stack for You

- `Flutter` mobile app
- vision model/API
- local saved history with optional sync later

---

## 11. Real Risks

- plant disease accuracy is hard
- recovery advice can be noisy
- users need trust and clarity

So version 1 should be positioned as:

- guidance
- not certainty

---

## 12. Best First Positioning

### Weak

- AI plant helper

### Better

- garden plant health diagnostic

### Stronger

- find out what may be hurting your garden plants and what to try next

---

## 13. Recommendation

This is a decent idea if you want a plant niche, but it is narrower and more useful than broad plant-ID alone.
