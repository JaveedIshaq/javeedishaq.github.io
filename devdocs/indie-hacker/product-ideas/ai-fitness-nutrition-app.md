# AI‑Fitness & Nutrition Flutter Template | Workout & Meal Planner UI Kit

**Overview**
- A full‑featured Flutter template for building AI‑powered fitness and nutrition applications, including workout planning, meal tracking, and health dashboards.
- Developed with **Dart (Flutter)** for Android and iOS.
- Designed to accelerate development of apps that provide AI‑driven coaching, personalized workout/meal recommendations, and comprehensive wellness tracking.

**Key Features**
- **AI Coach & Recommendations** – AI‑based workout suggestions, form analysis, and personalized nutrition plans.
- **Workout & Activity Tracking** – Log exercises, set goals, monitor daily challenges, and view progress charts.
- **Nutrition Dashboard & Meal Planner** – AI‑generated meal plans, calorie tracking, water intake, sleep monitoring, shopping lists, and recipe screens.
- **User Management** – Authentication, profile handling, and settings screens.
- **UI/UX Design** – Modern, clean interface with smooth transitions, multiple font/color themes, dark mode support, and responsive layouts for phones and tablets.
- **Customization** – Well‑structured codebase allowing easy modification of text, colors, graphics, and images.
- **Offline Access** – Local storage for workouts, meals, and AI chat history.
- **AI Chat Assistant** – Embedded chatbot for fitness and nutrition advice.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter)
- **Backend:** Configurable for Firebase or custom REST API.
- **Design:** Professional UI/UX with animation controllers and mediation modules.
- **Compatibility:** Supports Android and iOS platforms; responsive for various screen sizes.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the template from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. Configure required permissions (e.g., storage, internet) and set up your backend (Firebase or API).
4. Run `flutter pub get` and launch with `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Add gamified challenges and leaderboards for community engagement.
- Integrate with wearable devices for real‑time activity and health data.
- Expand AI capabilities to include diet restrictions, allergy alerts, and macro‑nutrient optimization.
- Offer subscription‑based premium content such as advanced coaching modules.
