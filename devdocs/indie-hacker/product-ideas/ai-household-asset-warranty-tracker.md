# AI Household Asset & Warranty Tracker
### Product Concept for a Solo Product Engineer

> **Simple promise:** Scan appliances and receipts once, then stop forgetting warranties, manuals, and maintenance tasks.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

People buy appliances and electronics, then lose:

- the receipt
- the warranty details
- the manual
- the maintenance schedule

This is a practical household utility with clear value.

---

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary users:

- homeowners
- renters with multiple devices
- families managing household appliances

Secondary users:

- landlords
- property managers
- home organizers

---

## 3. The Problem

Important asset information gets lost after purchase.

Users need:

- one place to store asset details
- warranty expiry reminders
- manual access
- maintenance reminders

---

## 4. Product Promise

### One-line version

- Track home appliances, warranties, receipts, and maintenance in one place.

### Stronger version

- Scan household devices and receipts to automatically organize warranty dates, manuals, and maintenance reminders before something breaks or expires.

---

## 5. Core Product Outcome

The user should be able to:

- create a home asset record
- save proof of purchase
- track warranty end dates
- get maintenance reminders
- quickly find manuals and details

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### MVP input

- receipt photo
- serial number
- product name
- purchase date

### MVP output

- organized asset profile
- warranty reminder
- maintenance reminder
- saved receipt/manual reference

### MVP workflows

1. User adds appliance or electronics item
2. App extracts product and purchase info
3. App stores the item and creates reminders

---

## 7. Feature Roadmap

### Version 1

- asset list
- receipt capture
- warranty date tracking
- maintenance reminders
- notes and photos

### Version 2

- serial number extraction
- manual lookup
- room-based organization
- export/share

### Version 3

- family sharing
- replacement part suggestions
- retailer/service provider integrations

---

## 8. Why This Product Could Work

This is a boring but real problem.

That is good.

It also has a stronger practical value story than many pure AI apps because:

- the reminders matter
- the stored records matter
- the AI is just there to remove setup friction

---

## 9. Monetization

- free tier with item limit
- one-time pro unlock
- affiliate revenue for parts/services

---

## 10. Best Stack for You

- `Flutter` mobile app
- local-first with optional `Supabase` sync later
- OCR/AI extraction layer

### Honest recommendation

This is a strong mobile-first utility idea.
It matches your strengths better than many high-risk AI concepts.

---

## 11. Real Risks

- if setup is too manual, users drop off
- manual lookup of manuals can be messy
- reminder reliability must be strong

So onboarding and quick-add flow matter a lot.

---

## 12. Best First Positioning

### Weak

- AI home asset app

### Better

- household warranty and maintenance tracker

### Stronger

- never lose warranty dates, receipts, or appliance reminders again

---

## 13. Recommendation

This is one of the better practical app ideas in the list.

It is useful, believable, and easier to monetize than many hype ideas.
