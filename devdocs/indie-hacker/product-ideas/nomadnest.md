# NomadNest — Work-Optimized Housing for Digital Nomads

## Core Problem
Digital nomads and remote workers face isolation and inefficiency finding short-term, work-friendly housing. They waste hours on fragmented platforms like Airbnb without vetting for productivity needs: reliable WiFi, quiet spaces, proper desk setups, and community vibes.

## Tech Capability Used
- **Crowdsourced Verification** — User-verified WiFi speeds and workspace quality
- **Geolocation APIs** — Real-time location-based recommendations
- **ML Matching** — Preference learning for personalized listings
- **AR Preview** (Post-MVP) — Virtual workspace tours

## Target Users
- Remote professionals and freelancers (25-45 years old)
- Tech, creative, and consulting workers
- Those traveling 3-6+ months per year
- Digital nomad hubs: Bali, Lisbon, Mexico City, Tbilisi, Chiang Mai

## Key Features (MVP)

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Work-Verified Listings | Filtered by WiFi speed, desk setup, noise levels |
| Real-Time Availability | Integrated booking calendar |
| Community Chat | Location tips and local networking |
| Work Score Rating | Productivity-focused review system |
| Timezone Optimization | Find places that fit your work hours |

## Post-MVP Features
- AR virtual workspace tours
- Expense tracker for tax deductions
- Networking events calendar (tied to user locations)
- Co-living community matching
- Visa and legal info integration

## Why This Works Now
**Without NomadNest:** Hours of Airbnb scrolling, bad WiFi surprises, isolation.

**With NomadNest:**
- "Show me places in Lisbon with 100+ Mbps, standing desk, quiet neighborhood"
- Community-verified: "WiFi actually held up during video calls"
- "3 other developers are staying in your building — want to connect?"
