# ContractGuard — AI Legal Review for Freelancers

## Core Problem
Freelancers sign contracts they don't understand. Lawyers cost $300/hr. Red flags get missed until it's too late — bad payment terms, unlimited liability, IP transfers.

## AI Capability Used
- **LLM** — Legal document analysis and risk extraction
- **Long Context Processing** — Handle 50+ page contracts
- **Comparative Analysis** — Benchmark against fair market terms

## Target Users
- Freelancers
- Independent contractors
- Small agency owners
- First-time founders
- Content creators signing brand deals

## Key Features (MVP)

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Instant Risk Summary | Upload → key concerns in 60 seconds |
| Clause Highlighting | Visual markup of problematic language |
| Plain English | Jargon → understandable explanations |
| Counter-Language | Suggested revisions for bad terms |
| Market Comparison | "This is unusual" vs "This is standard" |

## Why AI Makes This Work
**Without AI:** Expensive lawyer or risky YOLO signing.

**With AI:** Identifies "unlimited liability" buried in paragraph 47 of a 30-page MSA. Explains what "indemnification" actually means for YOU.

Scales legal review to anyone, not just those who can afford lawyers.

## Technical Considerations
- Secure document handling (encryption, deletion policies)
- Multi-format support (PDF, DOCX, images)
- Jurisdiction awareness (US, UK, EU variants)
- Explicit disclaimers ("not legal advice")
