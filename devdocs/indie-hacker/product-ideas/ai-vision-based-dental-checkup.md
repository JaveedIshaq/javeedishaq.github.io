# AI Vision-Based Dental Checkup
### Product Concept for a Solo Product Engineer

> **Simple promise:** Help users notice visible dental issues early and decide whether they should seek professional care.

---

## 1. Product Thesis

People ignore small oral health issues until they become painful or expensive.

An app that helps users do simple visual checks could create value, but only if positioned carefully.

This is not a dentist replacement.
It is early-warning triage.

---

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary users:

- adults managing basic oral hygiene
- parents monitoring family dental routines
- users with limited access to fast dental care

Secondary users:

- dental clinics for lead generation

---

## 3. The Problem

Users do not know when visible signs are worth acting on.

They need:

- basic guidance
- visual screening help
- hygiene reminders

---

## 4. Product Promise

### One-line version

- Check for visible dental warning signs before problems get worse.

### Stronger version

- Use guided photo checks to spot possible visible oral health issues and know when it may be time to book a dentist visit.

---

## 5. Core Product Outcome

The user should receive:

- visible risk guidance
- simple hygiene suggestions
- urgency level
- next-step recommendation

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### MVP input

- guided mouth photos
- symptom checklist

### MVP output

- visible concern categories
- low/medium/high caution note
- care suggestions
- dentist recommendation prompt

---

## 7. Feature Roadmap

### Version 1

- photo guidance
- symptom checklist
- simple visual screening
- oral care reminders

### Version 2

- progression history
- child/adult modes
- clinic referrals
- educational modules

### Version 3

- tele-dentist referrals
- dentist review mode
- regional care partner network

---

## 8. Why This Product Could Work

The user pain is real and recurring.

But this category is high-trust and high-risk.

The product only works if it stays careful and honest.

---

## 9. Monetization

- premium oral health tracking
- affiliate/referral for clinics
- care-plan upsells

---

## 10. Best Stack for You

- `Flutter` mobile app
- vision-capable API or model
- local history and reminders

---

## 11. Real Risks

- medical sensitivity
- false reassurance
- false alarms
- compliance and policy concerns

This is not an easy first indie-hacker product.

---

## 12. Best First Positioning

### Weak

- AI dentist app

### Better

- visible oral health screening helper

### Stronger

- a guided visual oral check to help you notice problems earlier, not replace a real dentist

---

## 13. Recommendation

Strategically, this is interesting but risky.

I would not make this one of your first products unless you have a strong reason.
