# AI‑Powered Pet Health Tracker Flutter App | Pet Care & Wellness

**Overview**
- A comprehensive Flutter app for tracking pet health, wellness routines, and receiving AI‑driven care insights.
- Built with **Dart** (Flutter) for Android and iOS devices.
- Focuses on proactive pet care through image recognition, health logs, and automated care schedules.

**Key Features**
- **AI Pet Recognition & Screening** – Scan pet photos to identify breeds and analyze visible health indicators.
- **Health Dashboard** – Centralized view of vaccine history, growth charts, and medical records.
- **AI Care Assistant** – Chatbot for answering pet care questions and providing breed‑specific advice.
- **Routine Management** – Customizable schedules for feeding, walking, medication, and grooming.
- **Pet Diary** – Log daily observations, behaviors, and memorable moments.
- **Offline Capabilities** – Access health history and care plans locally without internet.
- **Multi-Pet Support** – Manage profiles for multiple pets within a single account.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter).
- **Backend:** Firebase or configurable REST API for data sync; local SQLite/Hive for offline data.
- **UI Design:** Friendly, engaging interface with smooth animations and customizable themes.
- **Dependencies:** Camera libraries, AI/ML kit integrations, and chart rendering packages.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. specific: Configure AI API keys and set up local storage preferences.
4. Run `flutter pub get` and launch with `flutter run`.
