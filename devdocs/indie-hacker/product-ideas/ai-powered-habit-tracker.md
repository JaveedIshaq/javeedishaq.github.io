# AI‑Powered Habit Tracker Flutter App Template

**Overview**
- A Flutter template for building a habit‑tracking app with AI‑enhanced features, aimed at daily routine management and productivity improvement.
- Developed with **Dart** (Flutter 3.x) for both Android and iOS platforms.
- Includes a polished UI/UX with smooth transitions, responsive design, and modern visual aesthetics.

**Key Features**
- **Authentication** – User sign‑up/sign‑in flow.
- **Habit Creation & Management** – Add, edit, delete habits; set recurrence, reminders, and goals.
- **Daily Tracking** – Log habit completion, view streaks, and receive AI‑generated habit suggestions.
- **AI‑Powered Insights**
  - *AI Habit Suggestions* – Recommend new habits based on user behavior.
  - *AI Mood Detection* – Detect user mood from inputs and adjust habit recommendations.
  - *AI Sleep & Wellness Insights* – Provide sleep and wellness tips.
  - *AI Streak Prediction* – Forecast future streaks and motivate users.
- **Analytics Dashboard** – Visualize habit statistics, streaks, and productivity metrics.
- **Additional Modules** – Meditation, hydration, sleep, stress tracking, and emotional journal.
- **Customization** – Clean, modular codebase with easy theming, color, text, and graphic adjustments.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter 3.x compatible)
- **UI Design:** Modern layout with gradient accents, micro‑animations, and responsive components.
- **Dependencies:** Standard Flutter packages plus AI inference SDK (e.g., TensorFlow Lite or cloud‑based endpoint).
- **Supported Devices:** Android phones/tablets and iPhone/iPad.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. Run `flutter pub get` to install dependencies.
4. Connect a device or start an emulator and execute `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Replace the AI model with a custom endpoint or on‑device model.
- Integrate with third‑party health APIs for richer data.
- Add AR or gamification elements to boost user engagement.
