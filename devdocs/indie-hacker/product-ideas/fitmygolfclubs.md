# FitMyGolfClubs – AI Golf Club Equipment Analysis Flutter App

**Overview**
- A Flutter application that uses AI to analyze golf club equipment, providing fitting recommendations and performance insights.
- Built with **Dart** (Flutter 3.x) for Android and iOS platforms.
- Offers onboarding, club scanning via smartphone camera, results, history tracking, club comparison, and a premium subscription for detailed analytics.

**Key Features**
- **Club Scanning** – Capture photos of golf clubs; AI identifies make, model, shaft weight, kick point, loft, and adjustability.
- **Equipment Analysis** – Generates a comprehensive score, suggests optimizations, and compares clubs within the bag.
- **History & Dashboard** – Track past scans, view trends, and monitor equipment changes over time.
- **Premium Tier** – Unlocks detailed recommendations, multiple bag tracking, and performance validation (subscription $9.99/mo or $96/yr).
- **Customization** – Clean, modular codebase with easy theming, color, text, and graphic adjustments.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter 3.x compatible)
- **UI Design:** Modern, detail‑oriented layout with gradient accents and micro‑animations.
- **Dependencies:** Standard Flutter packages plus AI inference SDK (e.g., TensorFlow Lite or cloud‑based endpoint).
- **Supported Devices:** Android phones/tablets and iPhone/iPad.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. Run `flutter pub get` to install dependencies.
4. Connect a device or start an emulator and execute `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Replace the AI model with a custom endpoint or on‑device model.
- Integrate with professional fitting services via API.
- Add AR overlays to visualize club specifications on the scanned image.
