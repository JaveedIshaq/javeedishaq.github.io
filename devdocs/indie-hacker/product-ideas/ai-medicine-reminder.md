# AI Medicine Reminder — Scan Prescription, Never Miss a Dose

## Core Problem
Medication adherence is a massive health crisis. 50% of patients don't take medications as prescribed. Existing reminder apps require manual entry — tedious and error-prone. Elderly patients struggle with complex regimens.

## AI Capability Used
- **Vision API** — Prescription OCR and medical document understanding
- **LLM** — Extract dosage, timing, frequency, and special instructions
- **On-Device ML** — Smart reminder scheduling and pattern learning

## Target Users
- Patients with chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, etc.)
- Elderly individuals managing multiple medications
- Caregivers managing medications for family members
- Post-surgery patients with temporary complex regimens
- Anyone who struggles with medication consistency

## Key Features (MVP)

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Prescription Scan | Photo of prescription → all meds auto-added with timing |
| Smart Reminders | Alerts at optimal times, learns your routine |
| Refill Tracking | Warns before you run out of medication |
| Dose Logging | One-tap confirm, track adherence history |
| Caregiver Sharing | Family members can monitor (with permission) |

## Why AI Makes This Work
**Without AI:** Manual entry = errors, skipped setup, abandoned app.

**With AI:**
- Blurry prescription photo → perfect medication schedule in 30 seconds
- Understands "Take 1 tablet twice daily with food" → morning + evening reminders aligned with meal times
- Doctor's handwriting → parsed correctly
- "You usually take this at 8am, but you're traveling to a new timezone" → auto-adjusted

## Defensibility Analysis

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Data Moat** | Medication history is personal and persistent |
| **Switching Cost** | High — all meds need re-entry elsewhere |
| **Big Tech Risk** | Apple Health has reminders, but no prescription scanning |
| **AI Cost Viability** | ✅ Excellent (one-time scan cost) |
| **Retention** | Excellent — daily necessity for health |

## Technical Considerations
- Prescription format handling (varies by country, pharmacy)
- Medical terminology understanding
- Drug interaction warnings (API integration with drug databases)
- Accessibility (large fonts, voice prompts for elderly)
- HIPAA compliance for US market

## Competitive Landscape
- **Medisafe** — Popular but manual entry
- **Round Health** — Beautiful design, no scanning
- **Apple Health** — Basic reminder, no prescription intelligence
- **CVS/Walgreens apps** — Pharmacy-specific, limited

**Gap:** Prescription scanning + intelligent scheduling + family management.
