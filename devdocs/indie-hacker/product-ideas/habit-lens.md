# Habit‑Lens: AI‑Enhanced Wellness Coach

| **Section** | **Details** |
|---|---|
| **App Name** | **Habit‑Lens** |
| **Core Problem** | Users start health habits but lack personalized feedback and motivation; generic habit trackers don’t adapt to individual progress. |
| **AI Capability Used** | LLM for personalized coaching, sentiment analysis on journal entries, and recommendation generation. |
| **Target Users** | Health‑conscious individuals, people trying to build new habits (exercise, meditation, sleep). |
| **Key Features (MVP)** | 1️⃣ Daily habit logging (text, voice).<br>2️⃣ AI‑generated feedback & suggestions based on trends.<br>3️⃣ Predictive nudges (e.g., “You’re likely to skip tomorrow—try a 5‑min walk”).<br>4️⃣ Progress visualizations.<br>5️⃣ Community streak leaderboard (optional). |
| **Why AI Makes This Work** | Tailored coaching requires understanding user‑specific language, mood, and pattern—LLMs excel at this, unlike static advice. |
| **Monetization Strategy** | • **Free**: basic habit tracking.<br>• **Premium** $7.99 /mo for AI coaching, voice‑to‑text entry, and advanced analytics.<br>• **One‑time purchase** of “AI Coach Pack” for lifetime access (price based on projected AI usage). |
| **Marketing & Growth Angle** | • Influencer partnerships in fitness/wellness.<br>• Content series on “AI‑powered habit formation”.<br>• Free 14‑day trial of premium coaching. |
| **Why Now?** | LLMs are now capable of nuanced, empathetic responses at low cost; health‑tech market is booming and users expect intelligent personalization. |
