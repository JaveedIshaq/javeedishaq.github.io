## Overview
This is a comprehensive Flutter application designed for asset identification and tracking. It leverages AI to scan and identify assets, includes an AI chat assistant, and provides tools for managing asset history and tracking progress.

## Key Features

### 1. AI Capabilities
- **AI Asset Scanner:** intelligent scanning of asset images for quick identification.
- **AI Asset Chat Assistant:** An integrated chatbot to answer queries related to assets and providing assistance.

### 2. Asset Management
- **Detailed Tracking:** View asset history, detailed information, and insights.
- **Progress Tracking:** Screens for tracking progress and breaking down status analysis.
- **Inventory Management:** Suitable for monitoring and managing inventory efficiently.

### 3. User Interface & Experience (UI/UX)
- **Modern Design:** Features a contemporary, clean-coded design with smooth transitions.
- **Responsive:** Optimized for both Mobile (Android/iPhone) and Tablet devices.
- **Customizable:** Easy to modify text, colors, graphics, and photos to suit branding needs.
