## Overview
AI Room Remodeling is a Flutter template that provides AI‑driven interior‑design and home‑renovation assistance. It leverages **Google Gemini AI** to analyse rooms, suggest layouts, and generate smart remodeling recommendations.

## Key Features
- **AI Integration** – Powered by Google Gemini for high‑accuracy room analysis and design suggestions.
- **Onboarding & AI Room Scanner** – Guided onboarding flow followed by an AI‑enabled scanner that captures room dimensions and visual data.
- **Room History & Results** – Stores scanned rooms locally and displays detailed design outcomes.
- **Design Gallery & Inspiration** – Curated gallery of design ideas and room‑type inspirations.
- **Analytics Dashboard** – Provides insights into design trends, space utilisation, and potential red‑flags.
- **Local Storage** – All scans, history, AI suggestions, and gallery favourites are saved on‑device; no backend required.
- **Modern UI/UX** – Professional, responsive UI with smooth transitions, Font Awesome & Material Icons, multiple font and primary‑color options, and full tablet‑mobile responsiveness.
- **Cross‑Platform** – Works on Android and iOS devices.
