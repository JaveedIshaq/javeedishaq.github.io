# AI Fertility Tracking Flutter App | Ovulation & Period Tracker

**Overview**
- A Flutter application for comprehensive women’s health tracking, including ovulation, menstrual cycles, and fertility forecasting.
- Built with **Dart** (Flutter) for Android and iOS platforms.
- Leverages AI to provide personalized health insights, cycle predictions, and assessments.

**Key Features**
- **Cycle & Ovulation Tracking** – Calendar storage of period dates, flow intensity, and ovulation windows.
- **Fertility Forecast** – AI‑driven predictions for fertile days to assist with pregnancy planning or prevention.
- **Symptom & Mood Logging** – Track daily symptoms, mood fluctuations, and well‑being indicators.
- **AI Health Assistant** – Chatbot capable of answering questions about cycle health and general wellness.
- **Health Insights** – Personalized reports on cycle regularity, hormone balance indicators, and sleep/stress correlations.
- **Secure Data** – Local or cloud storage options ensuring privacy for sensitive health data.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter).
- **Backend:** Firebase (recommended) or configurable REST API.
- **UI Design:** Clean, feminine aesthetic with soft color palettes and intuitive navigation.
- **Dependencies:** Flutter packages for calendar, charts, and local storage; AI SDKs for prediction logic.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. specific: Configure backend services (e.g., Firebase) for user data synchronization.
4. Run `flutter pub get` and launch with `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Integrate with wearables (Apple Health/Google Fit) for temperature and heart rate data.
- Add community features for anonymous peer support.
- Expand the AI model to include pregnancy tracking once conception is confirmed.
