# Happy Hollow – AI Therapy for Kids & Teens

**Overview**
- A Flutter app designed to provide AI‑powered emotional support and therapy conversations for children and teenagers.
- Built with **Dart** (Flutter) for Android and iOS.
- Integrates **Google Gemini AI** to act as an "AI Therapist Friend," offering age‑appropriate guidance, mood tracking, and mental wellness exercises.

**Key Features**
- **AI Therapy Chatbot** – Uses Google Gemini to facilitate supportive, empathetic conversations tailored to the user’s age.
- **Age Selection** – Customizes the experience and content for different developmental stages (kids vs. teens).
- **Mood Tracking & Journaling** – Tools for users to record emotions and reflect on their day.
- **Wellness Tools** – Assessments, breathing exercises, grounding techniques, daily affirmations, and calming sounds.
- **Creative Outlets** – Drawing gallery and interactive activities to express feelings non‑verbally.
- **Dashboard & Stats** – Visual history of mood trends and usage statistics.
- **Offline Access** – Saved activities and history are available without an internet connection.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter).
- **Backend:** Firebase (for auth/database) + Google Gemini API (for AI).
- **UI Design:** Calm, child‑friendly interface with soft colors and "Happy Hollow" thematic elements.
- **Dependencies:** Standard Flutter packages, Firebase SDKs, and Gemini AI integration.

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. specific: Configure Firebase project (add `google-services.json` / `GoogleService-Info.plist`).
4. Set up Google Gemini API key in the app configuration.
5. Run `flutter pub get` and launch with `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Customize the AI persona to have different "character" voices.
- Add parental controls or a separate dashboard for guardians.
- Expand the library of therapeutic exercises and gamified wellness challenges.
