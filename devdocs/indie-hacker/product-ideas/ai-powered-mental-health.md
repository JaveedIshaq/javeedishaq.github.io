# AI‑Powered Mental Health Flutter App Template | Mindfulness & Wellbeing

**Overview**
- A comprehensive Flutter template for building a mental health and wellness platform.
- Developed with **Dart** (Flutter 3.x) for Android and iOS.
- Combines AI‑driven therapy tools with traditional wellness features like meditation, mood tracking, and health journaling.

**Key Features**
- **AI Core Capabilities**
  - *AI Chatbot* – 24/7 support for mental health queries.
  - *AI Plan Generator* – Personalized wellness plans based on user profiles.
  - *AI Motivation & Reframing* – Tools for cognitive reframing and daily motivation.
- **Wellness Modules**
  - *Mindfulness* – Meditation library, breathing exercises, and sleep stories.
  - *Tracking* – Logs for mood, stress, sleep quality, and physiological metrics (e.g., heart rate).
  - *Journaling* – Structured journals for gratitude, emotions, and daily reflection.
- **Community** – Social feed for peer support and shared experiences.
- **Analytics** – Dashboards visualizing mental health trends over time.

**Technical Details**
- **Language:** Dart (Flutter 3.x).
- **UI Design:** Professional, calming aesthetic with smooth transitions and responsive layouts.
- **Dependencies:** Standard Flutter packages, AI SDK integration framework, and font/icon libraries (Font Awesome, Material).

**Getting Started**
1. Purchase and download the source from CodeCanyon.
2. Extract the zip and open the project in Android Studio or VS Code.
3. Run `flutter pub get` to install dependencies.
4. Connect a device or start an emulator and execute `flutter run`.

**Customization Ideas**
- Integrate biofeedback from smartwatches for real‑time stress detection.
- Add gamification elements (badges, streaks) to encourage consistent practice.
- Connect with professional therapist directories for escalation of care.
