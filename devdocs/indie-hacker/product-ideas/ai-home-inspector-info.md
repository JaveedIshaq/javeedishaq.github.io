## Overview
AI Home Inspector is a Flutter template designed for home inspection and maintenance tasks. It leverages **Google Gemini AI** to analyze property images and provide intelligent inspection reports, maintenance suggestions, and issue detection.

## Key Features
- **AI‑Driven Inspection** – Uses Gemini AI to evaluate photos of rooms, identify potential problems (e.g., water damage, structural issues), and generate detailed inspection reports.
- **Maintenance Scheduler** – Allows users to create and track maintenance tasks, set reminders, and view a calendar of upcoming work.
- **Report Generation** – Generates downloadable PDF/HTML reports summarizing inspection findings and recommended actions.
- **Property History & Analytics** – Stores inspection history, tracks changes over time, and provides analytics on property health.
- **Modern UI/UX** – Polished, responsive design with smooth animations, customizable themes, and support for both phones and tablets.
- **Local Storage** – Offline caching of inspection data, reports, and schedules using `shared_preferences`/SQLite; no backend required.
- **Cross‑Platform** – Built with **Flutter 3.x** (Dart) and runs on Android and iOS.
- **Customization** – Clean, layered codebase allowing easy modification of UI elements, colors, icons, and AI parameters.

## Technology Stack
