# Week 12: Application Push And Interview Execution

## Objective

Convert 90 days of focused work into applications, interviews, and credible market positioning.

## Main Outcome

By the end of this week, you should have:

- final resume and LinkedIn positioning
- curated portfolio proof
- targeted applications sent
- interview answers ready for real conversations

## Work Tasks

- finalize resume around:
  - `Product Engineer`
  - `Flutter + Next.js/React + TypeScript + NestJS + PostgreSQL/Supabase + AI Integration`
- finalize LinkedIn headline and summary
- publish or organize portfolio case studies
- create a target company list
- apply selectively to roles that match:
  - product engineering
  - TypeScript full-stack
  - AI-enabled SaaS products
  - mobile + web crossover where useful

## Positioning Rules

Emphasize:

- end-to-end shipping
- product ownership
- practical AI features
- mobile plus web plus backend range
- current focus on TypeScript full-stack product systems

De-emphasize:

- Angular
- WordPress/CodeIgniter legacy work
- generic "AI enthusiast" language

## Interview Prep Focus

Have final answers ready for:

- Tell me about yourself
- Why this stack?
- Why Product Engineer?
- Tell me about `plantUSA rebuilt properly`
- Tell me about `NurseLife`
- Tell me about `CarPool`
- How do you use AI in products responsibly?
- When would you choose Supabase vs NestJS?
- When would Python actually be necessary?

## End-Of-Week Deliverables

- final resume
- final LinkedIn summary
- portfolio case study published or prepared
- target company list
- applications sent

## Final Standard

If after 12 weeks you still cannot clearly explain your product, architecture, AI workflow, and stack transition, then you did not prepare properly.

If you can explain them clearly and show working proof, you are credible enough to compete.
