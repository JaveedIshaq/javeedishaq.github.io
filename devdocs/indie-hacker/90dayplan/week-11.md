# Week 11: Mock Interviews And Gap Patching

## Objective

Stress-test your story, architecture knowledge, and stack credibility before sending serious applications.

## Main Outcome

By the end of this week, you should have:

- weak spots identified
- missing gaps patched
- stronger speed and clarity in answers

## Work Tasks

- run mock interviews for:
  - backend architecture
  - Next.js/React product feature discussion
  - PostgreSQL/data modeling
  - behavioral round
  - AI product design
- collect every question you fail or answer weakly
- patch only the top gaps
- improve project docs where your explanations are muddy

## Study Focus

- review only failed topics
- review only unclear architecture explanations
- tighten technical vocabulary where needed

## Interview Prep Focus

You must be able to defend:

- NestJS request lifecycle
- validation and auth flow
- Postgres indexing choices
- retrieval and answer-generation workflow
- why your AI feature is useful
- why your stack direction is intentional

## Optional Mini-Project

Only if you have time and a real gap:

- add one small agent feature
- example: draft next care actions based on diagnosis results and plant history
- include logging and basic evaluation notes

Do not start a second major product.

## End-Of-Week Deliverables

- mock-interview notes
- gap list
- patched weak areas
- improved case-study clarity

## Red Flags

- turning mock failures into another month of passive study
- inventing fake experience to cover gaps
- starting a new large build to avoid interviews
