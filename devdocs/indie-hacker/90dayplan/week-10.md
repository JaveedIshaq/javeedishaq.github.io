# Week 10: Behavioral Stories And Portfolio Case Study

## Objective

Package your experience so interviewers remember a shipper, not a stack collector.

## Main Outcome

By the end of this week, you should have:

- a polished project case study
- a sharp "tell me about yourself" answer
- leadership and ownership stories tied to real work

## Work Tasks

- write the `plantUSA rebuilt properly` case study:
  - problem
  - target user
  - architecture
  - tradeoffs
  - AI feature
  - lessons
- write short case-study versions for:
  - `NurseLife`
  - `CarPool`
- write your interview intro
- write five STAR-style stories:
  - ambiguity
  - architecture decision
  - technical conflict or tradeoff
  - scope reduction
  - production issue or failure

## Study Focus

- storytelling for engineering interviews
- concise technical explanation
- product outcome framing

## Interview Prep Focus

Rehearse:

- Tell me about yourself
- Tell me about a system you designed
- Tell me about a time you had to cut scope
- Tell me about a technical decision you defended
- Why are you transitioning toward TypeScript full-stack product roles?

## End-Of-Week Deliverables

- polished main case study
- draft portfolio content
- interview intro script
- five behavioral stories

## Red Flags

- sounding like a course learner instead of a builder
- over-explaining the tools and under-explaining the problem
- hiding behind years of experience instead of shipped proof
