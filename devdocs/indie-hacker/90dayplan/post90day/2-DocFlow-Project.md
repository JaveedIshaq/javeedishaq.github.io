### DocFlow Project

One more strong project, with a similar rank and the same job-first purpose:

**`DocFlow AI` — Document Intake, Review, and Approval Console**

This is not a consumer scanner app. It is a **workflow product** for teams handling documents that need extraction, review, triage, and approval.

## Why this is a strong second project
It matches what high-paying teams are buying and hiring for right now:
- document intelligence
- workflow orchestration
- human-in-the-loop AI
- admin dashboards
- audit trails
- reliability over hype

It also fits your target stack perfectly:
- `Next.js/React` for ops dashboard
- `NestJS` for workflow/backend

- invoice intake and approval
- contract/document triage
